The house

We do not grow our flowers. We remember them.

Elysian is a perfume house without an address. Its garden appears in the hour between two dreams — six acres of impossible colour, open dusk to dawn, gone by morning. Whatever survives the waking, on a sleeve or in a lock of hair, is carried to the atelier and pressed into glass.

This is not manufacture. It is testimony.

— The Head Gardener
