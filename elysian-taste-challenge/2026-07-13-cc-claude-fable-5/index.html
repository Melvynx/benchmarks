Chapter II

The Scholars

Our founders read until the mountains turned pink. This was not a metaphor; the mountains simply could not help themselves. Every Elysian object begins this way — with attention so complete that the landscape becomes embarrassed and reveals what it was hiding.

They kept no ledgers. They kept weather. The house archive holds four hundred years of borrowed afternoons, filed by temperature, and one thunderstorm that is overdue and accruing interest.

“Luxury is not rarity.
Luxury is a thing that should not exist,
existing anyway.”

— House maxim, carved somewhere soft
