# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Design-conscious visitors discovering a fictional premium botanical fragrance house. They arrive to feel transported, explore the house's point of view, and choose a scent by instinct rather than by shopping filters.

## Product Purpose

Elysian presents nocturnal botanical fragrances as living places. Success means the visitor remembers the visual world, interacts with the scent ritual, and feels compelled to enter the collection.

## Brand Personality

Lacquered, sensorial, uncanny. The voice is composed and intimate, with the theatrical confidence of a private conservatory opened after midnight.

## Anti-references

Generic luxury black-and-gold pages, startup hero templates, beige editorial minimalism, perfume ecommerce grids, frosted-glass card stacks, and ornamental motion without narrative purpose.

## Design Principles

- Let the garden behave like a place, never a backdrop.
- Create desire through atmosphere before explanation.
- Reward touch, movement, and curiosity with meaningful visual changes.
- Keep the copy tactile, specific, and brief.
- Preserve clarity and control beneath the surrealism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, descriptive image text, and a complete reduced-motion mode. Never rely on color alone to communicate the active scent.
