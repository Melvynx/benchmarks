# Design System

## Direction

Midnight conservatory viewed through lacquered cobalt glass, with petals lit like stage costumes. A drenched color strategy uses blue-black as the environment, wet moss as the botanical anchor, and electric coral as the living counterpoint.

## Color

- Night: `oklch(0.105 0 0)`
- Cobalt: `oklch(0.24 0.1 255)`
- Moss: `oklch(0.3 0.096 140)`
- Petal: `oklch(0.72 0.18 28)`
- Pollen: `oklch(0.9 0.14 95)`
- Mist: `oklch(0.95 0.018 220)`
- Muted ink: `oklch(0.76 0.025 220)`

## Typography

Display typography uses a high-contrast Bodoni or Didot system stack with disciplined, sculptural line breaks. UI and body copy use Avenir Next or a modern humanist sans serif. Scale is fluid and capped at 6rem.

## Shape and Layout

Image crops are botanical apertures rather than cards: circles, vertical slices, and full-bleed fields. The grid is asymmetric on desktop and becomes a strong single-column sequence on mobile. Corners stay mostly sharp, with organic geometry reserved for the image world.

## Motion

Motion resembles breath and light passing over leaves: slow scale drift, pointer parallax, a movable lens, and crossfades during scent selection. Reduced motion removes tracking and continuous movement while retaining immediate state changes.

## Components

- Slim fixed masthead with typographic monogram
- Full-viewport botanical stage with interactive lens
- Circular image aperture with orbiting copy
- Scent triptych controlled by tactile tab buttons
- Marquee-style botanical lexicon
- Full-bleed closing invitation
