# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Curious, design-literate visitors meeting Elysian for the first time on a quiet screen, usually with sound off and a few minutes to wander. They are not looking for a feature checklist. They are looking for a point of view they can feel.

## Product Purpose

Elysian is a fictional premium house that collects rare atmospheres: still lifes, landscapes, interiors, and the small rituals that make a place feel invented. The landing page should make the house memorable in one minute and reward a slower second look through movement and discovery.

## Brand Personality

Imaginative, exacting, and a little uncanny. The voice is intimate and observant, with the confidence of a house that has existed just out of sight. It invites rather than pitches.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic startup hero layouts, interchangeable SaaS cards, beige editorial minimalism, default luxury black-and-gold clichés, and gallery pages that reduce artwork to tidy rectangles. Avoid copy that sounds like a manifesto generator.

## Design Principles

- Make the first impression feel like entering a physical room, not opening a product page.
- Let artwork behave like material: crop it, overlap it, and give it a little impossible geometry.
- Keep the copy sparse, specific, and sensory enough to create a second image in the visitor's mind.
- Give every interaction a sense of turning a page, changing a lens, or opening a drawer.
- Keep the experience legible and calm even when the composition becomes strange.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and controls. All interactive elements must be keyboard reachable with visible focus, imagery needs useful alternative text, and reduced-motion preferences must disable parallax and long transitions without hiding content.
