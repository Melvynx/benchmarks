# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and model evaluators comparing coding-agent benchmark runs. They work across several generated applications at once and need to understand quality, cost, speed, token usage, and agent behavior without losing the visual comparison.

## Product Purpose

Benchmark Compare turns local benchmark evidence into a decision surface. It should make differences between models and harnesses obvious, preserve access to raw evidence, and keep generated applications as the primary visual focus.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, analytical. The interface should feel trustworthy and fast, with dense information presented through clear hierarchy rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic analytics dashboards, repeated metric-card grids, walls of missing values, oversized metadata tables, decorative developer-tool styling, and controls that compete with benchmark content.

## Design Principles

- Lead with comparison decisions, then expose evidence on demand.
- Keep generated applications and meaningful metrics visually dominant.
- Distinguish recorded facts from estimates and assumptions.
- Preserve raw transcripts, commands, IDs, and paths without making them the default view.
- Use one consistent vocabulary for models, harnesses, effort, tokens, time, and cost.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, complete keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable at 200% zoom.
