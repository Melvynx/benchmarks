## React Doctor warning triage

- The evidence fetch is intentionally on demand. Preloading every transcript in
  the route loader would transfer very large historical transcripts that most
  sessions never open.
- Generated apps are same-origin fixtures and require scripts plus same-origin
  access for runtime fidelity and synchronized scrolling. The iframe now has a
  curated sandbox that removes top navigation and download privileges; the
  detector deliberately flags the compatibility tradeoff.
- Remaining maintainability and pnpm-hardening warnings are repository-level
  architecture or dependency-policy items, not interface defects introduced by
  this audit.
