Free-plan thumbnails now ship with a small **thumbfa.st** wordmark in the bottom-left corner. Upgrade to **any paid plan** (Pro, Ultra, or Poweruser) to remove it.

## What changed

- Free generations now include a small thumbfa.st watermark in the bottom-left
- Behind the scenes we keep BOTH versions: a clean original and a watermarked copy
- The day you upgrade, every existing thumbnail you made on the free plan instantly switches to the clean version - no regeneration needed

## Want clean exports?

**Upgrade to any paid plan** and your thumbnails come out unbranded. No watermark, no logo, just your image.

A new "Remove watermark" button is now visible in the editor on free accounts, one click away from the upgrade flow.

## Why

Thumbfa.st pays real costs (OpenAI / Gemini API calls, R2 storage, etc.) on every free generation. The watermark gives the free tier a clear identity and gives paid users a tangible reason to upgrade beyond just credits.
