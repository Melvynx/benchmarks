Three new features are now live to help you generate better thumbnails and earn free credits.

## Referral Program — Earn Free Credits

Share your referral link and earn **1 free credit** for every user who signs up through it and completes at least one generation.

- Go to your profile and copy your unique referral link
- Share it on Twitter, YouTube, or anywhere your audience is
- Every qualifying referral automatically adds a credit to your account — no limit

## Inspiration Anonymization

When saving an inspiration thumbnail, you can now anonymize it to avoid AI content policy blocks caused by real faces.

Two options are available when adding an inspiration via URL:

- **Create** (0.1 credits) — saves the inspiration as-is for style and composition reference
- **Anonymize** (1 credit) — processes the image and replaces any detected character with a neutral placeholder silhouette

The placeholder acts as a positional guide for the AI: it preserves the pose, framing, and composition of the original while removing the identity. When you generate a thumbnail using an anonymized inspiration, the AI places your reference person exactly where the placeholder was — producing much more accurate and consistent results.

## Community Inspiration Library

The inspiration library has been expanded with a large collection of curated community thumbnails. All of them come pre-anonymized with the new placeholder system, so you can use them immediately without worrying about policy issues.

Browse the inspiration gallery, pick a layout you like, and generate.
