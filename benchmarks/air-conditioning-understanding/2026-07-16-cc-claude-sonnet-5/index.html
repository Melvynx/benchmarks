The core idea: air conditioning does not create cold — it moves heat from one place to another using a sealed refrigerant loop.
Cooling: indoor coil absorbs heat from room air → compressor → outdoor coil releases that heat outside.
Heating: a reversing valve flips the loop — the outdoor coil absorbs heat from outside air, the indoor coil releases it into the room.
