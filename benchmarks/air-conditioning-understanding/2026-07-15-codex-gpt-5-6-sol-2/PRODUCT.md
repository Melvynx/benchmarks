# Product

## Register

product

## Users

First-time viewers exploring how reversible air conditioning works on desktop or mobile. They need to compare systems and modes without prior HVAC knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Make heat-pump behavior spatially obvious: air conditioning moves heat, refrigerant stays in a sealed loop, indoor and outdoor air stay separate, and heating reverses the heat-exchanger roles.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, calm. The experience should feel like a museum engineering exhibit with the legibility of a professional technical instrument.

## Anti-references

Avoid classroom diagrams, generic glass dashboards, toy-like machinery, decorative particle noise, and labels that obscure the scene.

## Design Principles

- Explain through synchronized motion before relying on prose.
- Preserve the physical boundaries between air streams and refrigerant.
- Make every color, particle, and control carry a stable meaning.
- Keep the machinery cinematic while the interface stays quiet.
- Reveal complexity in short replayable steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard and touch operation, clear focus states, reduced-motion behavior, redundant shape and text cues beyond color, and a useful fallback when WebGL is unavailable.
