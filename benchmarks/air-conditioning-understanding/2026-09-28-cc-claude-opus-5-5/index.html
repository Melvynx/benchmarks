How reversible air conditioning works

Cooling: the indoor coil is very cold, so it absorbs heat from room air. The compressor pumps that heat through a sealed refrigerant loop to the outdoor coil, which releases it to outside air.

Heating: a reversing valve swaps the flow. The outdoor coil now absorbs heat from outside air, even when it's cold, and the indoor coil releases it into the room.

Split vs monoblock: a split system uses separate indoor and outdoor units joined by refrigerant lines. A monoblock keeps the whole circuit in one indoor cabinet and pulls outdoor air through two wall ducts.
