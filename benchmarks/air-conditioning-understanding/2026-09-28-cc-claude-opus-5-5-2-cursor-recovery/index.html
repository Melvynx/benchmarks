The interactive 3D exhibit needs WebGL. Here is the idea it shows:

  1. Air conditioning moves heat; it does not create cold.
  2. Refrigerant circulates in a sealed loop between two heat exchangers. It absorbs heat where it evaporates and releases it where it condenses.
  3. The compressor raises pressure and temperature; the expansion valve drops them.
  4. Indoor air and outdoor air never mix. A split system uses two units joined by refrigerant lines; a monoblock keeps the whole circuit indoors and ducts outdoor air through the wall.
  5. In Heating mode the reversing valve swaps the roles of the two coils, so heat is pulled from outdoor air and released indoors.
