In short: an air conditioner moves heat instead of making cold. A sealed refrigerant loop absorbs heat in one coil and releases it in the other. A compressor raises the refrigerant's pressure and temperature, and an expansion valve drops them again. In Heating mode a reversing valve swaps the two coils' roles, so heat is pulled from outdoor air and released indoors. Indoor and outdoor air never mix.
