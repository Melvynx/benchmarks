Guided Explanation · Step 1 of 6
Moving Heat Rather Than Creating Cold
Air conditioners are heat pumps. Refrigerant circulates in a sealed copper loop, absorbing heat energy from indoor room air and releasing it outdoors—while indoor and outdoor air never mix.
