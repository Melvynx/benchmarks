# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People moving quickly through a mixed-volume inbox on desktop or mobile. Their immediate jobs are to identify what needs attention, read a thread, organize it, and write a response without losing context.

## Product Purpose

Provide a credible local mail client that makes a seeded mailbox feel active and manageable. Success is a fast, familiar flow from search or navigation, to reading, organizing, composing, and sending.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, capable. The interface is familiar enough to disappear into everyday mail work, with one warm-red identity cue used for clear actions and active states.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, oversized rounded cards, low-contrast gray text, overloaded color, and fake controls that do not change state.

## Design Principles

1. Let the message list carry the density, not visual ornament.
2. Make state changes immediate and recoverable.
3. Keep primary mail actions within reach of the current context.
4. Preserve familiar inbox conventions while making small-screen use deliberate.
5. Use color to signal selection and action, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, high-contrast body copy, touch-sized controls on mobile, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Target WCAG AA contrast for normal text.
