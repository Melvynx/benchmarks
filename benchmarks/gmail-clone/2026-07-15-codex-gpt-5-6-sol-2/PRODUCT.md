# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing a busy personal and professional inbox across laptop and mobile. They need to scan, find, read, organize, draft, and send email quickly without learning a new mental model.

## Product Purpose

Provide a convincing local email client that preserves the clarity, density, speed, and familiar workflows of a modern inbox. Success means a user can move from discovery to reading, organizing, composing, and sending with state changes reflected consistently throughout the product.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, composed, precise. The interface should feel calm under information pressure, responsive to every action, and polished without distracting from mail.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard cards, oversized type, low-density layouts, novelty navigation, glass effects, excessive rounding, generic AI gradients, and controls that look interactive but do nothing.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the mailbox scannable at a glance.
2. Make every meaningful action immediate, reversible where practical, and consistent across views.
3. Preserve familiar mail conventions while refining hierarchy and feedback.
4. Let content carry the interface; chrome stays quiet and efficient.
5. Maintain one coherent workflow across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard access. Preserve visible focus, clear labels, touch targets on mobile, reduced-motion behavior, and redundant signals for states that otherwise depend on color.
