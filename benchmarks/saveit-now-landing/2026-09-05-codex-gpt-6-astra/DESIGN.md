# SaveIt.now campaign redesign

## Visual system
Warm ivory canvas, near-black ink, vivid burnt-orange accents drawn from the supplied campaign. Geist is chosen for its confident, tightly tracked oversized display forms and clear interface text. Wide cinematic imagery alternates with generous editorial whitespace. Thin rules and purposeful pill actions replace a generic card grid.

## Image audit and art direction
All six 2048 × 1148 images were opened and visually inspected before implementation.

| Image | Visual audit | Purpose and crop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| portal-ridge.png | Saturated coral sky, central luminous doorway, strong leading path | Hero: a doorway back to your interests. Wide crop preserves the central portal, left-aligned copy stays clear of it. |
| observatory.png | Pale sky, illuminated arc over a building, figure and lush hillside | Workflow: a visual metaphor for the agent seeing and understanding. Central-right focus. |
| lake.png | Coral mountain reflected in water, evergreen framing | Interactive example bookmark: a place worth saving, then finding through remembered meaning. |
| portal-rocks.png | Pale lavender doorway framed by vivid rocks and a path | 100% promise: the route back remains the central visual. Portrait crop keeps the doorway. |
| portal-arch.png | Tall luminous arch, flowers and warm dusk | iOS: a pocket-sized portal to the same collection. Artwork, not a fabricated app screen. |
| home.png | Small illuminated house, winding path, abundant negative sky | Closing conversion: literal home for bookmarks, completing the journey. Keep the house visible right of copy. |

## Motion and interaction
Short entrance animation only, tactile button feedback, native anchor navigation, user-controlled save/find demonstration, accessible disclosure FAQ and mobile menu. All animation disabled with reduced motion. No auto-playing carousel.

## Asset delivery
The original PNGs remain unchanged. The page uses quality-82 JPEG derivatives at the original dimensions, reducing the six-image transfer payload from roughly 45 MB to roughly 6.5 MB. The hero is prioritized and all below-fold campaign imagery is lazy loaded.
