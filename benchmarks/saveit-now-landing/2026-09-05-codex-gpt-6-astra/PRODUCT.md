## Purpose and audience
Your home for bookmarks. For people who collect interesting links and want to find them again without manual filing. Agentic capture, understanding, organization, and retrieval connect to a polished iOS experience.

## Direction
Cinematic, energetic, confident. Sell rediscovery before features. Use concise English without em dashes. The 100% message is an ambition, not a measured success rate or guarantee. No invented testimonials, urgency, or app screenshots.

## Accessibility
Semantic landmarks, keyboard interactions, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support.
