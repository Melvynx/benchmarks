# Baseline provenance

This Vite app isolates the SaveIt.now landing page as it existed at Git commit
`88ee367`, immediately before the landing redesign commit `2c15af4`.

The baseline intentionally retains the dark page and orange-gradient hero. The
six images under `public/images/landing-v2/` came from the original
`landing_v2/` art folder and are supplied as raw redesign material.

Image inventory:

- `observatory.png`: hilltop observatory with a glowing arc
- `lake.png`: coral-lit mountain lake
- `home.png`: small illuminated house on a flowered hill
- `portal-arch.png`: glowing arch among rocks and flowers
- `portal-ridge.png`: glowing rectangular portal on a ridge
- `portal-rocks.png`: pale portal framed by rocks and vegetation
