# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who save links constantly across browsers and phones, then lose them in folders, tabs, and forgotten lists. They want retrieval confidence without manual filing.

## Product Purpose

SaveIt.now is your home for bookmarks. You only save. The product captures, understands, categorizes, organizes, and retrieves. The iOS experience keeps saving and rediscovery immediate wherever you are. Success is the belief that what you save will come back when you need it.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, confident, immediate. Campaign energy with editorial craft. Warm portal-and-landscape art direction. Voice is clear and conversion-focused, never vague, never hype-stat padded.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS purple gradients. Cream-and-terracotta template landings. Broadsheet newspaper grids. Dark-mode glow kits. Card-stacked feature grids. Fake app screenshots. Em dashes. Invented testimonials, guarantees, or urgency.

## Design Principles

1. Imagery carries meaning: each campaign frame earns a job in the story.
2. Sell the lived transformation before listing features.
3. One promise per section; keep rhythm short and specific.
4. Motion supports presence and hierarchy; respect reduced motion.
5. Brand first: SaveIt.now stays recognizable from hero through footer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast as baseline. Keyboard-reachable navigation and FAQ. Prefer `prefers-reduced-motion`. No reliance on color alone for meaning.
