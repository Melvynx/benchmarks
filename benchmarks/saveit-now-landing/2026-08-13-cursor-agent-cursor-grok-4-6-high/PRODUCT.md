# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who save links all day (researchers, builders, writers) and later cannot find them in folders, browser bars, or read-later piles. They meet the page on the web, often from a phone, wanting one reason to start.

## Product Purpose

SaveIt.now is your home for bookmarks. You save. The agent captures, understands, categorizes, organizes, and retrieves. Success is the feeling that a save will come back when you ask, on the desktop and in a native iOS flow.

## Brand Personality

Luminous, alpine, decisive. Campaign-film confidence: dusk meadows, a house lamp, a doorway of light. Direct second person. No hype adjectives, no fake proof.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS card grids, dark-orange gradient heroes, image galleries, Klim-style editorial scaffolding, fake app screenshots, invented stats or urgency, cream/sand marketing pages.

## Design Principles

- One promise: just save it, then find it again.
- Campaign stills are chapters, not decoration.
- Mechanism before feature lists.
- Motion is cinematic and optional; reduced motion still works.
- Contrast and tap targets are part of the craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard FAQ, skip link, 44px targets, `prefers-reduced-motion`, semantic landmarks, no information by color alone.
