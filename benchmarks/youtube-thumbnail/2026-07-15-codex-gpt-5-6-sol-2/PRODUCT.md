# Product

## Register

product

## Users

YouTube creators and creative teams working in a focused editing session who need to turn a written concept and visual references into thumbnail options quickly.

## Product Purpose

Generate one to four 16:9 YouTube thumbnail concepts from a prompt, optional supporting images, one visual inspiration, and optional person references. Success means the complete workflow is obvious, fast, recoverable after errors, and produces downloadable results without leaving the page.

## Brand Personality

Focused, cinematic, precise. The interface should feel like a quiet creative tool, with the generated work carrying most of the visual energy.

## Anti-references

Avoid busy AI dashboards, chat-style generation flows, decorative gradients, glass panels, oversized marketing copy, excessive cards, and interactions that hide important controls behind menus.

## Design Principles

- Keep the creative brief and its references visible as one continuous workflow.
- Make every upload state clear before, during, and after interaction.
- Reserve strong color for primary actions and current selections.
- Preserve user inputs and successful results when generation fails.
- Let generated thumbnails dominate the results area.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain visible keyboard focus, clear labels, sufficient contrast in both themes, 44-pixel touch targets where practical, non-color-only state cues, polite live-region updates, and reduced-motion behavior.
