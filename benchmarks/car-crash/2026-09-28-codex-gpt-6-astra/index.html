About the experiment

Dry-stacked masonry: individual rigid bricks with no mortar. Depth is represented by parallel 2D slices: bricks collide within their own layer, and every layer exchanges momentum with the same 1,250 kg car. Layers are slightly offset in the drawing to reveal thickness. The car accelerates over its approach, then loses its drive at first contact. Ground friction slows the wreck. Settings immediately reset the scene. Physics runs at 240 Hz; heavy scenes may run slower than real time to preserve accuracy. The camera fits the track and follows the car after impact.
