Model & fixed specifications

1,250 kg vehicle · 4.2 × 1.45 m collision envelope. Each brick is 0.50 × 0.24 m. World scale: 80 px/m, with responsive camera zoom. Depth layers occupy parallel 2D planes: each layer has brick-to-brick contact and all layers exchange impulses with the same vehicle. No mortar or artificial break animation. The launch rig accelerates to the chosen speed; propulsion disengages on first wall contact. This is an illustrative rigid-body experiment, not a structural safety model.
