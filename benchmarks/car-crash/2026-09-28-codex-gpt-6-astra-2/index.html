Inside the experiment

Individual rigid bricks, gravity, rotational inertia, friction and contact impulses. A powered test rig accelerates the car to its target; propulsion cuts at first impact and the tires brake against the track.

Depth is represented by overlapping 2D planes. Bricks contact neighbors in their own plane; every plane contacts the same car and ground. Each layer adds its full mass. The camera fits the viewport without changing the physical scale.

This is dry-stacked masonry: no mortar, fracture or structural bonding. The car is a rigid collision body. Dust is cosmetic. Fixed inputs and time steps reproduce the same physical state; playback speed only changes viewing time.
