# Cowdie

A cozy, playful 3D virtual pet built with React, TypeScript, Three.js, and Vite.

## Run

```sh
pnpm install
pnpm dev
```

The application runs on **http://localhost:9190**. Both development and production preview use port 9190 with strict port checking.

```sh
pnpm check
pnpm build
pnpm preview
```

## Your little meadow

- Select an apple, carrot, or strawberry and feed your friend. The treat travels to her mouth, disappears as she munches, and improves fullness.
- Tap the cow or the heart button for affection. Play brings a bouncing ball and happy hops; baths bring bubbles; rest changes the meadow into a starry night.
- Each care interaction builds your bond and creates a memory. Daily rituals encourage feeding, play, and affection.
- Your pet, needs, friendship, name, and memories are saved in this browser. Needs gently change over time, and sleep restores energy. Offline decay is capped to keep the experience kind.
- Settings offer renaming, optional gentle sound, and calmer motion. The app also respects the system's reduced-motion preference.

The scene is modeled and animated in real time, with no downloaded models or image assets. A WebGL-capable browser is required for the 3D meadow. Typography has system fallbacks when Google Fonts is unavailable.
