# Lumail launch film

Public research, September 30, 2026. No local Lumail projects inspected.

- https://lumail.io/ — current positioning, AI editor, agent integration, workflows, transactional email, unlimited subscribers, 3,000 free emails/month, no card.
- https://lumail.io/docs — product and audience: creators and small teams.
- https://lumail.io/docs/ai-integration — ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, CLI, API, SDK.
- https://lumail.io/about — simple, fast, creator-focused email marketing.
- https://webclaw.io/data/startups/company/lumail.io — public brand extraction dated August 28, 2026: #F7F8F8, #8A8F98, #0D0E10, #C9CCD1, #08090A. Inter, Geist, Geist Mono. Direct browser access was blocked in this workspace, so the palette is based on this secondary public extraction. Local system sans-serif and mono fonts substitute for the unavailable web fonts. Lumail is retained as a wordmark; the envelope is an illustrative email motif, not a claimed official logo.

## Direction

45-second monochrome launch film. Crisp whole-phrase motion, generous reading time, high-contrast oversized typography, animated product illustrations, moving data packets, and a final URL/offer hold. Illustrative product scenes do not represent exact screenshots. No fabricated performance metrics or customer counts.

0–5: Email without busywork. 5–9: Lumail brand reveal. 9–16: Document-like email composition. 16–23: AI drafts, segments, schedules; user control. 23–30: Always-running workflows. 30–36: Pricing on sends, unlimited subscribers. 36–40: Marketing and transactional email together. 40–45: Free plan and lumail.io.

Original procedural music and transition sound design; no third-party music.
