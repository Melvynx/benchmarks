## Creative direction

45 seconds, 16:9. A typographic product launch film. Near-black and porcelain, fine technical lines, confident grotesk typography, softly dimensional email objects. All feature UI is a purpose-built motion illustration, rather than a recording of a signed-in account. Avoid fabricated performance statistics or customer claims.

Story: rediscover email → Lumail reveal → AI-assisted writing → connect your agent → workflows and transactional sending → unlimited subscribers → free-plan invitation.

The current public site confirms 3,000 emails per month free, no card required; no per-subscriber billing; AI-assisted writing; agent connections; visual workflows; and transactional email alongside campaigns.
