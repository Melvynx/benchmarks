# Lumail launch film — public research

Checked 30 September 2026. No local Lumail project was inspected.

- https://lumail.io/ — current positioning: AI-assisted email marketing; document-style editor; agent-assisted drafting, segmentation and scheduling; automated sequences; transactional email.
- https://lumail.io/pricing — unlimited subscribers; billing by emails sent; free plan includes 3,000 emails per month without a card.
- https://lumail.io/docs/features — personalization, visual workflows, behavioral triggers, analytics, and sending queues.
- https://lumail.io/docs/ai-integration — agent integrations, CLI, SDK and Tools API.
- https://lumail.melvynx.com/chatgpt — ChatGPT prepares drafts and safe updates; its OAuth plugin does not send or schedule campaigns. The film's generic agent scene shows a prepared draft and retains human approval.

Creative direction: cream paper, dark navy, burnt orange, oversized sans-serif typography with an editorial serif accent, vector envelope imagery. Product views are illustrative compositions; sample copy and contacts are fictional. Brand artwork is reconstructed rather than an extracted official logo.

45-second structure: invitation, brand reveal, writing, agent workflow, automation, unified email, subscriber pricing, free tier, call to action.

Tooling constraints: the installed Portly launcher and dev-browser CLI were blocked in the workspace sandbox. The Treg brand cross-check was also refused by the tool's approval policy; no paid lookup was performed.
