# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visually curious visitors encountering Elysian as a fictional premium house. They arrive for atmosphere, discovery, and an experience worth remembering rather than a conventional product transaction.

## Product Purpose

Elysian is an interactive atlas of impossible places. The landing page turns four artworks into a navigable world and leaves visitors with the feeling that they briefly entered a private mythology.

## Brand Personality

Ceremonial, lucid, otherworldly. The voice is precise and sensuous, with a dry thread of mystery.

## Anti-references

Generic startup landing pages, boxed feature grids, beige luxury minimalism, passive image galleries, component-library chrome, and decorative motion without narrative purpose.

## Design Principles

- Let imagery behave like geography, not decoration.
- Give each interaction a consequence in the fictional world.
- Make mystery legible: unusual composition, clear navigation.
- Alternate spectacle with quiet detail.
- Preserve the visitor's agency through motion and reduced-motion support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, useful alternative text, comfortable touch targets, and a complete reduced-motion experience.
