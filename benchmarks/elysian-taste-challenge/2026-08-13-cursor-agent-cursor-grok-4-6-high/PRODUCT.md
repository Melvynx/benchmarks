# Elysian

## Register
brand

## Purpose
A private tasting house that plates four vanished geographies as a single evening. The page is the house: guests descend from a still-life cameo through nested landscapes into a temple night, then write for a seat.

## Users
People who collect rare evenings. They arrive already willing to be strange. The job is to make them feel they have entered a room that does not exist on a map, then let them request one of twelve seats.

## Personality
Ceremonial, alpine, gilt. Slightly surreal. Never boutique-hotel, never perfume-campaign black-and-gold cliché as the whole idea, never startup landing.

## Anti-references
Generic hero/features/CTA stacks. Cream paper editorial. Identical image cards. Curtain intros. Numbered section kickers as page grammar. Playfair / Cormorant / Fraunces.

## Principles
1. Images are apertures, objects, and nested worlds, never rectangular sections.
2. One signature: a camera pull-back through four stacked geographies.
3. Color is cobalt night with temple gold. Warmth lives in the photographs.
4. Copy is sensory and specific. No aphoristic rebuttals, no em dashes.
