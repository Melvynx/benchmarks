The statement

Elysian does not sell objects. We sell the four o'clock light of a place that has never been surveyed, the particular hush of a colonnade that was finished last Thursday and has stood for nine hundred years.

Each world leaves the workshop numbered, sealed, and slightly warm. We guarantee the marble, the gilding, and the behaviour of the sky. We do not guarantee the birds — the birds have their own arrangements.

Signed, the Atelier — in an unspecified antiquity
