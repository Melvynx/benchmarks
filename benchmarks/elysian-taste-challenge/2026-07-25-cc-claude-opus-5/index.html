Ⅰ. The question we are always asked

Where is Elysian? The honest answer is that Elysian is not somewhere. It is a temperature. It is the minute in late afternoon when a room quietly decides to be beautiful and tells nobody.

For two centuries this house has done one thing, slowly. We travel to places that do not exist, we stand in them until the light turns, and we bring back what cannot be photographed. What you are about to descend through is the fifth such work.
