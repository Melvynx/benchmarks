Grok 4.7

Elysian keeps four rooms that were never built.

A frame, a river, a head, and a door. They share a house and refuse one weather.

Drag sideways, or use the arrow keys, to pull the rooms apart. Home returns them to one window.

One window

The frame already held a mountain. The other rooms climbed in.

Plate I · Atelier

The table remembers

A gilt frame holds another landscape. The column is coral. The bust looks past you on purpose.

Plate II · Library

A head for weather

Two urns the color of pollen, a cloth the color of the sky’s other idea. The books under him are warmer than the volcano.

Plate III · Valley

The river practices gold

The peaks refused the season. A single tree did not. Coral shrubs carried the decision down to the water.

Plate IV · Night house

Leave the door for stars

The pediment is still arguing. Eight columns keep the minutes. Step through when the doorway is darker than the sky.
