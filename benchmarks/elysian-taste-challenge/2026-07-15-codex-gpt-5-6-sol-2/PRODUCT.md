# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Design-literate visitors encountering a fictional premium house for the first time. They arrive to feel transported, browse slowly, and discover a visual world with enough craft and mystery to reward attention.

## Product Purpose

Elysian is an imagined house of rare places and sensory rituals. The landing page exists as an encounter rather than a catalogue: it should establish a memorable mythology immediately, then invite visitors to uncover its four realms through movement and interaction.

## Brand Personality

Cultured, ecstatic, enigmatic. The voice is precise and poetic, never corporate, breathless, or ornamental for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Generic luxury ecommerce, beige editorial templates, conventional startup sections, component-library cards, literal image galleries, predictable scroll-reveal stacks, faux-classical ornament, and passive rectangular image placement.

## Design Principles

- Treat the page as one continuous world, not a collection of sections.
- Let imagery behave like portals, atmospheres, and objects with physical presence.
- Build tension between scholarly restraint and vivid natural color.
- Reward curiosity with responsive details that never obscure the core story.
- Make every transition feel composed, tactile, and intentional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for readable copy and controls. Preserve keyboard navigation and visible focus, use semantic landmarks, provide meaningful image alternatives, and offer a reduced-motion experience that retains the composition without parallax or continuous animation.
