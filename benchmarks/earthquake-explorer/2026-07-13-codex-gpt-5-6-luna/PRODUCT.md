# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Curious people, journalists, researchers, and travelers who need a fast, trustworthy view of recent global seismic activity. They may be checking a breaking event, comparing activity across a time window, or following one earthquake from the global map into the official record.

## Product Purpose

Seismic Observatory turns the live USGS earthquake catalogue into a calm, inspectable field instrument. It answers what is happening now, where the strongest recent events are, how a selected event is classified, and how activity is distributed through time. Success means a first-time visitor can orient in seconds, then move naturally from global pattern to one event without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, observant. The voice is plainspoken and technically literate without pretending to predict risk. The product should feel like a contemporary geological field notebook crossed with a well-made scientific instrument.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards with equal KPI tiles, arbitrary chart grids, rainbow severity markers, decorative gradients, excessive glass effects, fake activity, or emergency-alert language. Avoid old-terminal cosplay and avoid implying that magnitude alone predicts damage.

## Design Principles

- Show the live catalogue before asking people to configure it.
- Connect geography, time, and record detail so every representation reinforces the others.
- Treat uncertainty and missing data as part of the record, not as visual defects.
- Use semantic color sparingly, and pair it with text, labels, and shape.
- Make the map powerful but never the only path to an insight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. The core experience must work with keyboard and screen reader navigation, including a text alternative for every map interaction. Preserve visible focus, provide announcements for refresh and selection changes, support reduced motion, maintain readable contrast at 200% zoom, and keep touch targets at least 44px.
