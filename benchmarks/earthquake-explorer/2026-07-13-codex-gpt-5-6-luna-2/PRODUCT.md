# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Curious people, journalists, researchers, and travelers use the observatory to understand recent global earthquake activity quickly. They need a calm view of what is happening, where the strongest recent events are, and which events deserve a closer look without mistaking the product for an emergency alert service.

## Product Purpose

Seismic Observatory turns live USGS earthquake data into a legible geographic and temporal investigation surface. It makes the global picture useful within seconds, then lets a person filter by magnitude, depth, alert, tsunami flag, review status, and place before opening one event in context. Success means users can move naturally from overview to event and back without losing their selection or filters.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, field-ready. The voice is plain and transparent, with the confidence of a scientific instrument and the humanity of a well-kept field notebook. It states uncertainty and data age clearly, explains domain terms at the point of use, and never implies prediction or safety guarantees.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards made from equal KPI cards, decorative gradients, rainbow-coded markers, glassmorphism, dense tables that hide geography, and black-box alert language. Avoid emergency claims, invented damage estimates, fictional fallback events, and any control that appears functional but is not wired to the live dataset.

## Design Principles

- Geography is the primary reading surface. Every important list insight has a synchronized map path and every map-only insight has a non-map path.
- Time is something to scrub, not a footnote. The connected timeline identifies the same events across map, stream, and detail.
- Signal and uncertainty travel together. Magnitude, depth, alert level, review status, felt reports, and missing fields remain distinguishable.
- Progressive disclosure keeps the first five seconds calm while preserving a fast route to advanced filters and event evidence.
- Trust is visible. USGS attribution, refresh age, stale state, errors, and the official source link are part of the product surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. The primary flow is keyboard reachable, map insights are duplicated in the event stream, focus states are visible, status changes are announced, and severity never relies on color alone. Reduced motion is respected. Controls use readable labels and touch-friendly hit areas, and responsive layouts preserve the core map, timeline, filters, and event detail tasks at narrow widths.
