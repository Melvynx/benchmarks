# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Curious people, journalists, researchers, and travelers who need a fast, legible view of current global earthquake activity. They move between scanning the world, comparing recent events, and checking one event's official details.

## Product Purpose

Groundwave turns live USGS earthquake data into a coherent geographic and temporal view. Success means a first-time visitor can identify the strongest recent event within seconds, understand its time, place, depth, and reporting context, then narrow the dataset without losing their place.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, grounded. The product should feel like a contemporary seismic observatory: serious without alarmism, dense without becoming cryptic, and tactile without decorative nostalgia.

## Anti-references

Not a four-card analytics dashboard, emergency-alert product, old scientific terminal, rainbow marker map, glass panel collage, or decorative data visualization. Avoid fake precision, invented risk claims, and magnitude-as-damage shorthand.

## Design Principles

- Geography, time, and the event stream behave as one instrument.
- Lead with the strongest current signal, then disclose deeper controls progressively.
- Every visual encoding has a textual, keyboard-accessible equivalent.
- Show data provenance, revision status, missing fields, and freshness honestly.
- Use density to support comparison while preserving a calm reading order.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA, support keyboard-only operation and 200% zoom, respect reduced motion, provide 44px touch targets, visible focus, live announcements, and non-color cues for magnitude, depth, selection, and status.
