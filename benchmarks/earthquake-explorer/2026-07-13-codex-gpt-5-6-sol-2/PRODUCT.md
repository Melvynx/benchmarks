# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Curious people, journalists, researchers, and travelers who need to understand recent global earthquake activity quickly, then inspect the evidence behind any notable event.

## Product Purpose

Make live USGS earthquake data legible across geography, time, and significance. The default view identifies what deserves attention, while linked map, timeline, stream, and detail views support careful exploration without implying prediction or guaranteed safety.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, and grounded. It should feel like a contemporary field observatory: serious without alarmism, information-dense without becoming a terminal.

## Anti-references

Generic KPI dashboards, decorative command centers, rainbow severity markers, excessive glass, emergency-alert theater, and interfaces that treat magnitude as a damage forecast.

## Design Principles

1. Geography, time, and detail are one connected selection.
2. Show the evidence before interpretation.
3. Use emphasis sparingly so meaningful activity is unmistakable.
4. Preserve context through every transition.
5. Make every map insight reachable without a mouse.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA, support keyboard-only use and 200% zoom, provide textual equivalents for map and chart information, retain visible focus, use 44px touch targets, respect reduced motion, and never encode severity or state with color alone.
