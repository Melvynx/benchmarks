# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Remote workers, distributed teams, and digital nomads comparing working hours across cities. They need to understand a full day quickly, often while preparing a meeting or checking several working relationships.

## Product Purpose

Turn timezone arithmetic into an aligned visual schedule. Success means a user can identify viable shared hours, change the reference city or sleep schedule, and understand the result without doing mental conversion.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, worldly. The interface should feel dependable and quietly confident, with the data carrying most of the visual weight.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic world-clock dashboards, decorative maps, neon dark-mode control rooms, oversized marketing typography, and dense calendar interfaces that hide the core hour alignment.

## Design Principles

- Make alignment the primary interface.
- Reserve color for meaning.
- Keep timezone actions direct and reversible.
- Explain results in the reference city's language.
- Preserve clarity at both desktop and phone sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access. Never communicate availability by color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences, use explicit labels, and keep touch targets practical on mobile.
