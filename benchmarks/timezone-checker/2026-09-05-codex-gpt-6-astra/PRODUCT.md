# Timezone overlap checker

## Register
Product: a focused scheduling utility for distributed teams and digital nomads.

## Purpose
Make common waking hours instantly apparent, either for a whole team or relative to one base city. The supplied brief defines the complete interaction and feature scope.

## Design principles
Precise alignment, understated visual hierarchy, honest timezone information, accessible controls, and instant feedback. No accounts or network services required. User configuration stays in local browser storage.

## Personality
Considered travel-journal precision: calm, worldly, quietly personable. White surfaces, deep plum actions, soft green shared availability. Avoid dashboard clutter, decorative charts, and competing accents.
