# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Remote workers, distributed teams, and digital nomads who need to find a meeting hour that actually works, or compare their own day against colleagues in other cities.

## Product Purpose

A timezone overlap checker. The job on every visit: see, in one aligned 24-hour grid, which hours everyone is awake (Meeting) or which hours each person overlaps with a home base (Base). Success is an instantly obvious green band and a precise summary of the windows.

## Brand Personality

Precise, nautical, unhurried. Harbor-chart calm: instruments you trust, not a dashboard that performs.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS timezone widgets, World Clock list apps, cream-and-terracotta “warm tool” skins, near-black acid-green terminal clones, and anything that hides the grid behind cards or onboarding.

## Design Principles

- The grid is the product. Alignment is the feature.
- Overlap must read as one shape, not twenty-four decorated cells.
- Controls stay on the surface. No modal for a two-second setting change.
- Speak in hours and cities, never in offsets-as-the-headline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast. Keyboard-complete city picker and mode switch. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored. Overlap is color plus weight, not color alone. Touch targets ≥44px on mobile controls.
