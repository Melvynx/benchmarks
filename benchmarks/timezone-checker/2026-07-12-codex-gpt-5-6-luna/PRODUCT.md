# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Remote workers, distributed teams, and digital nomads checking a shared set of cities while choosing a meeting time or planning recurring collaboration.

## Product Purpose

Timezone Overlap is a compact planning tool that makes shared awake hours visible across a 24-hour reference-day grid. Success means a user can add a city, understand the overlap at a glance, and adjust the shared awake window without translating timezones by hand.

## Brand Personality

Focused, quietly technical, and reassuring. The interface should feel like a dependable workbench for a decision that needs to be made quickly.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-dashboard clutter, decorative world-map imagery, generic calendar SaaS patterns, low-contrast pastel labels, and dense timezone jargon that makes the tool feel like an engineering console.

## Design Principles

- Show the decision before the configuration. The overlap summary and aligned grid are the main surface.
- Keep the reference city visible. Every hour is anchored to one readable frame of reference.
- Make state semantic. Sleep, awake, and overlap should be distinct without relying on color alone.
- Keep configuration reversible and immediate. Mode and awake-window changes should never need a save step.
- Respect the user's context. Responsive layouts should be purpose-built for touch and reduced motion should be honored.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for meaningful text and controls. Provide visible focus states, descriptive labels, status text in addition to color, keyboard-accessible city search and removal controls, and a reduced-motion fallback. Support both 12-hour and 24-hour time formats.
