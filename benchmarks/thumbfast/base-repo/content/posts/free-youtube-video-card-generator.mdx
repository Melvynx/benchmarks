## What is the YouTube Video Card Generator?

The YouTube Video Card Generator is a free tool that lets you create pixel-perfect YouTube-style video cards. Whether you need them for b-roll footage, presentations, blog posts, or social media content, this tool gives you full control over every detail.

## How It Works

Using the tool is straightforward:

1. **Paste a YouTube URL** to auto-fill the video title, channel name, and thumbnail
2. **Customize everything** — edit the title, channel name, views, duration, and published date
3. **Adjust the theme** — switch between light and dark presets, or fine-tune colors, font sizes, spacing, and border radius
4. **Toggle elements** — show or hide the duration badge, channel avatar, channel name, views, published date, and progress bar
5. **Export** — download as a high-quality PNG or copy directly to your clipboard

## Use Cases

### Content Creators

Create YouTube-style cards to showcase your videos in blog posts, newsletters, or social media. The cards look exactly like real YouTube thumbnails, making your content instantly recognizable.

Generate b-roll overlays that show YouTube video cards in your editing timeline. Perfect for reaction videos, commentary content, or video essays that reference other creators' work.

Add professional-looking video references to your slides. Instead of screenshots, use clean, customizable cards that match your presentation theme.

Share visually appealing video references on Twitter, LinkedIn, or Instagram. The customizable theme lets you match your brand colors.

The generator provides extensive customization:

- **Theme presets** — Light mode, dark mode, or fully custom colors

- **Color pickers** — Customize text, background, duration badge, and progress bar colors
- **Toggle switches** — Show or hide any element on the card
- **Progress bar** — Add a YouTube-style playback progress indicator

Every card you download or copy is automatically saved to your browser's local storage. You can restore any previous card configuration with a single click, making it easy to iterate on designs or recreate cards you made earlier.
