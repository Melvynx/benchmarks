## What a Video Card Is

A video card is the visual representation of a YouTube video: thumbnail, title, channel name, view count, duration badge, publish date. It's what YouTube itself shows when it recommends a video.

![Example of a generated YouTube video card](https://codelynx.mlvcdn.com/images/2026-02-22/clipboard_image_1771728831.png)

These are useful in a lot of contexts:

- **Social media posts** — Drop a card into a tweet, LinkedIn post, or Instagram story to reference a video without just pasting a URL.
- **Video editing b-roll** — Insert a video card as an overlay when you mention another video in your content. It looks clean and professional vs. a plain screen recording.
- **Thumbnails** — Use a card in your thumbnail design to create a "watch next" visual that stands out.
- **Presentations and docs** — Embed a proper-looking card instead of a screenshot.

## Everything Is Configurable

The tool gives you full control over every element of the card.

**Video data:**

- Thumbnail image URL
- Title
- Channel name and avatar
- View count
- Duration (shown as a badge over the thumbnail)
- Published date
- Progress bar position (0–100%) — useful for a "currently watching" look

**Theme:**

- Light or dark preset, or fully custom
- Card background and text colors
- Font size and spacing scale
- Border radius
- Duration badge colors
- Progress bar colors

**Display toggles:**

- Show or hide duration, views, published date, channel avatar, and channel title independently

You can combine these to match your brand, your video's style, or the platform you're posting to.

## One-Click Export
