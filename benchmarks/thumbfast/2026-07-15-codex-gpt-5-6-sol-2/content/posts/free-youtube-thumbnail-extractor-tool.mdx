## Available Thumbnail Sizes

YouTube stores thumbnails in multiple resolutions. Our tool extracts all of them:

| Resolution         | Dimensions | Best For                         |
| ------------------ | ---------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Max Resolution** | 1280x720   | Full-size usage, highest quality |
| **SD**             | 640x480    | Standard definition displays     |
| **HQ**             | 480x360    | Social media sharing             |
| **MQ**             | 320x180    | Previews and smaller displays    |
| **Default**        | 120x90     | Tiny thumbnails and lists        |

Not all videos have the max resolution version available — older or lower-quality uploads may only have SD and below. The tool handles this gracefully and shows you exactly which sizes are available.
