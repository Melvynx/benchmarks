Welcome to the official launch of Thumbnail.io — the AI-powered YouTube thumbnail generator that helps creators stand out.

## What's New

- **AI Thumbnail Generation**: Create stunning thumbnails from text prompts using Google Gemini and OpenAI GPT-Image
- **Person Manager**: Upload reference photos of yourself or collaborators to include them in generated thumbnails
- **Inspiration Library**: Save and organize inspiration thumbnails to guide your generation style
- **Credit System**: Flexible credit-based system — only pay for what you use
- **MCP Integration**: Generate thumbnails directly from Claude via the Model Context Protocol

## Getting Started

1. Upload a reference photo of yourself in the **Persons** section
2. Browse YouTube and save inspiration thumbnails to your **Inspirations** library
3. Head to the **Editor**, describe your thumbnail, and click **Generate**
4. Download and upload your favorite result to YouTube

We're just getting started. More features coming soon!
