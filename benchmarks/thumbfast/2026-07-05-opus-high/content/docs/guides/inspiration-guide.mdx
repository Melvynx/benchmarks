## What Are Inspirations?

Inspirations are reference thumbnails that tell the AI:

- **Color schemes** to use
- **Composition style** to follow
- **Visual elements** to incorporate
- **Overall aesthetic** to match

When you add an inspiration as a style reference, the AI will try to recreate a similar look with your content.

## Adding Inspirations

### From YouTube

1. Go to the **Inspirations** page
2. Click **Add Inspiration**
3. Paste a YouTube video URL
4. The thumbnail will be automatically extracted
5. Give it a memorable name

### Organizing Inspirations

Use descriptive names to easily find inspirations later:

- "Tech Review Style"
- "MrBeast Bold Colors"
- "Minimalist Dark Theme"
- "Reaction Face Closeup"

## Using Inspirations in Generations

### As Style References

When creating a thumbnail:

1. Go to the **Style References** section
2. Click **Add Style Reference**
3. Select one or more inspirations
4. The AI will match their visual style

### Multiple Style References

You can combine multiple inspirations to blend styles:

- Use 2-3 inspirations for balanced influence
- The AI will find common elements
- Great for creating unique combinations

## Best Practices

### Choose High-Quality Thumbnails

Select inspirations that:

- Have clear, high-resolution images
- Represent the style you want
- Have strong visual elements
- Are professionally designed

### Match Your Content Type

Use inspirations that match your content:

| Content Type | Inspiration Style                  |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Tech Reviews | Clean, product-focused             |
| Vlogs        | Expressive faces, bold text        |
| Tutorials    | Clear layouts, step indicators     |
| Gaming       | Dynamic, colorful, action-oriented |

### Build a Library

Create a diverse collection of inspirations:

- Different styles for different video types
- Various color schemes
- Multiple composition styles
- Seasonal or trending designs

## Tips for Better Results

### Study What Works

Analyze why certain thumbnails catch your eye:

- What colors dominate?
- How is the face positioned?
- Where is the text placed?
- What makes it click-worthy?

### Stay Current

Thumbnail trends change:

- Update your inspiration library regularly
- Follow successful creators in your niche
- Notice emerging visual patterns

### Don't Over-Reference

When generating:

- 1-2 inspirations usually work best
- Too many can confuse the AI
- Focus on the most relevant style

## Filtering Inspirations

The Inspirations page offers two views:

- **Mine**: Inspirations you've added
- **All**: All inspirations in your organization

This helps teams share and collaborate on style references.

## Summary

1. Add inspirations by pasting YouTube URLs
2. Name them descriptively for easy reference
3. Use them as style references when generating
4. Combine 1-2 for best results
5. Build a diverse library over time
6. Keep your collection updated with trends
