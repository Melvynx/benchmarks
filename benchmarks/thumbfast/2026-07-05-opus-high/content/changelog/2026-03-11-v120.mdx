Introducing **Tagging** — a faster way to reference your images, style references, and persons directly inside your prompt.

## How It Works

Type `@` anywhere in your prompt to instantly tag any asset from your library:

- **@Image** — embed a logo, icon, or any uploaded image directly in the prompt
- **@Style Reference** — apply the visual style of a saved inspiration
- **@Person** — reference a person from your person library

Tags appear as highlighted chips inline with your text, so you always know exactly what the AI will use during generation.

## Why Tagging Matters

Before tagging, you had to separately select images, style references, and persons from different panels before generating. Now everything lives in the prompt itself — write your description and tag assets as you go.

This makes complex prompts with multiple references much easier to compose and understand at a glance.

## Example

Instead of writing a generic prompt and hoping the AI figures it out:

> Person shocked hands on face, large logo floating, clean solid off-white background

You can now be explicit:

> Person shocked hands on face, large @Claude Logo floating, clean solid off-white background

The AI knows exactly which image to use and where to place it.
