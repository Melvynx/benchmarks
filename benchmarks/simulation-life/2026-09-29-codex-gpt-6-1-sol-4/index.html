A FIELD GUIDE TO YOUR LITTLE WORLD

Everything is connected.

Give your garden a nudge, then watch the story unfold. There’s no perfect outcome — just interesting ones.

Fruit is where it all starts.

Trees slowly grow fruit. A blue blob takes one bite, waits to finish eating, and turns that fruit into energy. The tree needs time to grow it back.

Blues are the garden’s gatherers.

They seek fruit, avoid crowds, and scoot away from reds. With enough stored energy, they spend 46 energy to create a little copy. Their baby starts with 32.

Reds follow the blue crowd.

A gentle contact takes energy from a blue and gives 82% to a red. Then the red pauses, and the blue gets an 8-second grace period. Low-energy blobs eventually fade.

Watch the charts: blues often grow first, and reds follow later. Change the fruit supply to see the ripple travel through the whole garden.
