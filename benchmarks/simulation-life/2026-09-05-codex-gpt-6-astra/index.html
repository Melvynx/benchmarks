A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

Everything is connected.

Trees slowly regrow fruit after a snack.

Blue blobs eat fruit, spend energy moving, and share stored energy with their babies.

Red blobs gently take energy from blues, then rest. Without enough blues, they fade.

Sliders change the living rules immediately. Starting populations, world size and seed apply when you reset. Presets start a fresh experiment.

Charts show actual counts, not scripted curves. Some gardens thrive; others settle or fade. That's part of the experiment.
