How it works

  • Blue blobs eat green fruit from trees. Fruit regrows slowly.
  • Red blobs bump blue blobs to sip their energy, then pause to digest.
  • Moving burns energy. A blob with lots of energy can split into a baby — never for free.
  • Too many reds → blues dip → reds go hungry → blues return. That loop makes the waves.
