**The bug:** on phone-sized screens, the legend and the guided-tour caption share the same spot. Opening the legend made the caption invisible and untappable, so if the tour started while the legend was open, its Prev/Next/Exit buttons couldn't be used. The fix in the responsive CSS keeps the tour caption on top while the tour runs:

```css
body.show-legend:not(.touring) #caption{opacity:0;pointer-events:none}
body.show-legend.touring #legend{display:none}
```
