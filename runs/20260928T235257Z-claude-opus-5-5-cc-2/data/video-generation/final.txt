**How it's built:** `src/stage.html` is an animation that runs on exact time values. Playwright captures it frame by frame and ffmpeg encodes the result. `src/audio.mjs` generates a 120 BPM soundtrack whose hits and whooshes line up with each scene change. Run `pnpm build` to regenerate everything (pnpm only). The `preview/` and `research/` folders are working files and can be deleted.

The chatcut and posthog connectors weren't used and need you to sign in before they'll work. For chatcut, run `/mcp` in an interactive session. For posthog, use your claude.ai connector settings.
