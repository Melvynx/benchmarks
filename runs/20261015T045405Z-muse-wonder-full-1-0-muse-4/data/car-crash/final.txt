The simulation is a real impulse-based rigid-body engine (SAT collision, friction, restitution, rotation, sleeping) stepped at 240 Hz so the car cannot tunnel through bricks, with dust, camera shake, skid marks, nose crumple, and a following camera that keeps the car and rubble field in frame. Browser verification found no console errors; the extreme corner (1,920 bricks at 45 m/s) runs slower than real time in a headless browser but remains stable.
