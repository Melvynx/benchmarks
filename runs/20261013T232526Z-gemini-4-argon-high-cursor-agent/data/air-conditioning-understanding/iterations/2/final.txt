Created a self-contained interactive 3D reversible air conditioning visualization in `index.html`.

### Key Features Implemented
- **Architectural Cutaway Scene**: Multi-layer insulated wall separating an interior living space (hardwood floor, sofa, coffee table, floor lamp, thermostat) from an outdoor patio and garden.
- **System Comparison (`Split System` vs. `Monoblock`)**:
  - **Split System**: High-wall indoor unit and outdoor condensing/heat-pump unit linked by a sealed copper refrigerant loop passing through a wall sleeve.
  - **Monoblock System**: Single indoor console housing the complete refrigeration circuit with an internal герметиc air partition barrier and twin through-wall outdoor intake and exhaust ducts.
- **Operating Modes (`Cooling` vs. `Heating`)**:
  - Animates the **4-Way Reversing Valve** to swap the evaporator and condenser roles between the two heat exchangers, reversing refrigerant flow direction, coil temperatures, and thermal transfer badges (`Heat Absorbed Here` / `Heat Released Here`).
- **Animated Flows & Callouts**: Distinct particle streams and legend for **Room Air**, **Outdoor Air**, **Hot Gas / Subcooled Liquid Refrigerant**, **Cold 2-Phase / Suction Vapor**, **Heat Energy Transfer**, and **Condensate Moisture**, paired with interactive 3D component callouts and a 6-step replayable guided explanation.
