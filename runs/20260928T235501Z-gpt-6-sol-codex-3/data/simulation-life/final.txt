Built the living-garden simulator in the current directory. It has a draggable round world, animated fruit trees and blobs, energy-based interactions, live controls, presets, blob inspection, and charts recorded from the simulation.

`pnpm run build` and JavaScript syntax checks pass. Simulation checks showed both groups active at day 480 in the balanced preset, rapid blue growth in the fruit-rich preset, and a blue dip followed by red decline in the red-heavy preset.

The app is configured for port **9178**. I could not complete browser verification because Portly failed to launch in this environment.
