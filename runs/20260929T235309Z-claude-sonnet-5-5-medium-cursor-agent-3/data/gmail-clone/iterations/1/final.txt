The app is finished. It builds cleanly and runs on port 9361 as a Portly-managed server (`sonnet-mail/web`). The browser title and the visible header title are both "Claude Sonnet 5.5".

**What it is:** a local Gmail-style client built with Vite, React and TypeScript. Nothing leaves the browser, and the mailbox is saved in `localStorage`.

**What's in it**
- **Mail data:** about 30 seeded threads with varied senders, timestamps, labels, attachments, multi-message conversations, and Spam, Trash and snoozed items.
- **Navigation:** Inbox with Primary, Promotions, Social and Updates tabs, plus Starred, Snoozed, Sent, Drafts, Scheduled, All Mail, Spam, Trash and user labels. You can create and delete labels.
- **Organizing:** select one, many or by filter; star, archive, delete, mark spam, mark read or unread, snooze and label. Destructive actions have Undo toasts.
- **Search:** operators such as `from:`, `label:`, `is:unread`, `has:attachment` and `in:`, a live preview dropdown, recent searches and match highlighting.
- **Reading:** collapsed earlier messages with the latest expanded, recipient details, attachment cards, reply, reply all, forward and smart-reply chips.
- **Compose:** contact autocomplete, Cc/Bcc, a formatting toolbar, attachments, autosaved drafts, schedule send, undo send and recipient validation.
- **Live behavior:** replies arrive a few seconds after you send, refresh delivers new mail, and snoozed and scheduled mail return or send on time.
- **Extras:** keyboard shortcuts (press `?` for the list), light, dark and system themes, three density levels, a signature setting, and a reset-demo-data option in the account menu.
- **Responsive:** on mobile the sidebar becomes a drawer, rows switch to a two-line layout, and compose goes full screen.
