**What was broken, and the fixes**
1. **Restitution fed energy back into pinned bricks.** A brick trapped between the car's nose and the road got its bounce re-applied every step, and its speed ran away (up to 122 m/s at the default settings). Bounce now applies only to fresh impacts (`arb.impact`). Brick speeds now peak at about 1.18× car speed, which is what a 0.18 restitution predicts.
2. **Wheels hitting debris used unbounded forces.** A wheel landing on a brick could push about 100 kN into a 2.5 kg brick in one step, which also launched the car. The tyre force is now an upward push plus grip at the contact patch, with a force cap. Tyres absorb half the height of a brick they roll onto (`envelope: 0.5`), have a separate lower cap on debris (`FmaxDebris`), and wheels only ride on debris directly under the tyre.
3. **The sloped lower nose worked as a ramp.** Flat bricks got wedged under the car and lifted it. The lower front is now a vertical plow face. At 45 m/s the car used to fly to 6 m; it now rides over rubble with about 0.35 m of lift.
4. **The result label was misleading.** Any brick that moved 5 cm counted against the wall, so every run said "Wall breached". A new "Fallen" count tracks bricks that dropped, toppled or got knocked out. The possible results are now "Wall held", "Wall shoved, still standing", "Partial collapse" and "Wall breached".

I also toned down the impact dust, which was hiding the car. And I added a compact layout, so on a narrow screen the telemetry cards no longer cover the road.
