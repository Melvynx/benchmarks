Built the complete earthquake explorer in the current directory.

Highlights:

- Live USGS feeds with 1-hour, 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day views.
- Interactive OpenStreetMap basemap with individually rendered, non-clustered points.
- Magnitude, depth, place, sorting, and visible-map-area filters.
- Synchronized event list, overlap cycling, detailed event drawer, geolocation, and USGS links.
- Responsive layout, accessible controls, reduced motion, and redundant color/size encoding.

Verification passed:

- `pnpm check`
- `pnpm build`
- UI anti-pattern scan
- [USGS daily GeoJSON feed](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/summary/all_day.geojson) confirmed live

Run with `pnpm dev` at `http://127.0.0.1:9154`.
