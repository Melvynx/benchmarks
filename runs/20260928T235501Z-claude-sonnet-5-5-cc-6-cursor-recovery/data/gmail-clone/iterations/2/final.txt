## What I actually verified
- **Static checks:** `tsc` is clean, vitest passes 16 of 16, and `pnpm build` succeeds. All three ran through `portly temp` and `portly wait` on the final code.
- **First run:** on a clean profile at 1440px and 390px, `document.title` and the visible `h1` both read exactly "Claude Sonnet 5.5". The mailbox seeds 150 threads and 27 are unread. There were no console errors or page errors.
- **Shortcuts:**
  - `j`/`k`/`Enter` open and move between threads.
  - `#` trashes a thread and `z` undoes it.
  - `l` opens the label picker and `b` opens the snooze dialog.
  - `/` focuses search.
- **Print:** with print media emulated, the header, sidebar and thread toolbar are hidden, and the subject and expanded message stay visible.
- **Earlier in the session (browser DOM checks):** selection and bulk actions, starring, archive, trash, labeling, search operators, compose with drafts and autosave, send and undo send, scheduled send, settings and themes, offline persistence, and the mobile drawer and floating compose button.

## Limitations
- **Not checked:** cross-tab sync, the Empty Trash dialog, the unsubscribe flow, unsnooze expiry, a visual pass on tablet, and 320px visuals. The 320px overflow sweep passed except the settings tab strip and clipped chips, which are intentional.
- **Screenshots:** I only got them back as text descriptions, so layout evidence is mostly DOM and computed-style assertions.
- **Bundle size:** the JS bundle is 597 kB (195 kB gzipped), which triggers Vite's chunk-size warning. The Material Symbols font is large too.
- **Data:** it is local-only, replies from contacts are simulated, attachments are stand-ins, uploads are capped at 2 MB, and links to `.example` hosts are intercepted.
- **Minor UX:** after `z` undo, the original "moved to Trash" toast stays visible next to "Action undone".
