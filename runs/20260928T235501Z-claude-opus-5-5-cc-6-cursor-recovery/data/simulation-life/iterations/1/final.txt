**What the previous session left:** the simulation engine (`src/sim.ts`), the settings and presets (`src/params.ts`) and some tuning scripts. The balanced preset was tuned; the other presets weren't, and there was no UI at all.

**What I finished:**
- **Preset tuning.** Fruit-rich used to boom and then wipe out both groups. It now uses a softer red payoff (contact 12, vision 4.5). Red-heavy starts with 26 reds and low-fruit uses stingier trees.
- **World view (`src/render.ts`).** A raised clay dish drawn in 3D-looking perspective. Drag to spin, vertical or Shift-drag to tilt, scroll to zoom.
  - Trees carry fruit on their canopies. A fruit disappears from the tree while a blue chews it, shrinks in the blob's hands, then regrows after the return delay.
  - Blobs are glossy ovals with faces. They hop, lean, blink, squash and stretch, and get worried eyes when fleeing, frowning brows when hunting, and "z"s when resting.
  - Eating, contact, births and fades each have short effects: sparkles, hearts, rings, puffs and floating energy numbers.
- **Charts and summary (`src/charts.ts`).**
  - The main timeline shows blobs on the left axis and ripe fruit on the right, with hover values.
  - A blue-vs-red phase loop and inherited speed/vision trends.
  - A run summary written from the recorded history, using cross-correlation to find which group leads and by how much.
- **Controls and inspector (`index.html`, `src/main.ts`, `src/style.css`).**
  - All 19 rule sliders plus a simulation speed slider (0.25× to 32×). Rule changes apply live; starting-world settings apply on reset, and the Reset button pulses when one is waiting.
  - Play, pause, step, reset and randomize buttons, the 5 presets, and +5 blue / +3 red buttons.
  - Clicking a blob opens an inspector with group, energy bar, age, current action, speed, sensing range, time left in any eating or pause animation, genes, generation, parent and number of babies. The map also shows its vision circle and a line to its target.
- **Title.** The browser title and the visible heading are both "Claude Opus 5.5".
