**What it is.** A Vite + React + Three.js dish you can drag to rotate. Blue and red blobs have energy: moving and waiting spend it, blues eat fruit, reds recover from contact that also costs the blue blob, and births spend stored energy so groups level off. Fruit shrinks when eaten and grows back. Charts, the phase plot, trait trends, and the story text all come from recorded history. Click a blob for color, energy, age, action, speed, sensing range, time left, and family.

The browser title and the only heading are **Grok 4.7**. Scripts use port 9352 (`pnpm dev`, `pnpm preview`).
