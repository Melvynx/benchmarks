**How it's built:**
- **Physics:** cannon-es with a fixed 1/120 s step, at most 6 steps per frame, and rendering interpolated between steps. Joint stiffness is reset for the 1/120 step, because cannon hardcodes 1/60 for constraints.
- **Car:** 20 rigid bodies:
  - cabin, two front-clip segments, two hood panels, trunk lid;
  - four doors, two bumpers, four wheels;
  - windshield, rear glass and four side windows.

  Joints break in stages when the solver's joint force, or the contact force on the part, passes a finite threshold:
  - Bumpers go from locked, to hanging by one corner, to free.
  - Doors go from locked, to swinging on a hinge, to torn off.
  - The hood has a fold hinge that buckles upward.
- **Crumple:** when a front-clip joint breaks, it becomes 8 springs that yield under compression and don't spring back. The segments can overlap, so the front visibly shortens. If they stretch too far apart, they tear free.
- **Wheels:** they roll as spheres while mounted. When the nose is pushed back into them, the hinge breaks and they become cylinders so they can fall flat.
- **Glass:** panes are replaced by 6 to 15 shard bodies that keep the pane's motion.
- **Wall:** 192 bricks by default, each its own body, in a staggered running bond. Both depth layers are real bricks and half-bricks are used at the ends. The wall is woken just before contact, because a sleeping brick acts as immovable for one step.
- **Rendering:**
  - clearcoat paint; reflective, transparent glass;
  - a 2048 soft shadow map that stays tight around the car and wall;
  - cheap contact-shadow blobs, and a sky environment generated in code;
  - ACES tone mapping, and dust, sparks, skid marks and camera shake as visual-only effects.
- **Controls:**
  - Pause/Play (Space), Replay (R), and 0.25× to 2× speed, which survives Replay and Apply.
  - Four cameras (keys 1–4): Impact is the default and Orbit rotates on its own until you drag.
  - All 17 settings rebuild the next run through Apply or Replay. The first run uses the mandatory defaults and starts by itself at 1×.

**Risks to check on first load:**
- **Thresholds:** they are estimated from momentum. If the default crash breaks too little or too much, tune the `thr` values in `buildCar`.
- **Friction:** cannon caps friction at μ·g·(the body's own mass), which ignores the load it carries. Stacked bricks therefore slide more easily than real ones would. For the mounted wheels I compensated with a 12× friction multiplier (`TIRE_LOAD`).
