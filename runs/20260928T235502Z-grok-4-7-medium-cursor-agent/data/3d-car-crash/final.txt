I'll build one self-contained crash page: real rigid-body breakup, a playback bar, and settings that rebuild the next run from the mandatory defaults.The rear chassis was long enough to overlap the cabin, and mass was applied with a Rapier call that would throw. Both would break the car before it reached the wall.`index.html` is a self-contained crash experiment. It starts on load at the mandatory defaults: 22 m/s, 1250 kg, a 12×8×2 brick wall, and the Impact camera at 1×.

The car is separate rigid bodies tied with breakable Rapier joints. On impact the front segments, hood, bumpers, doors, glass shards, and wheels come apart under contact, then keep colliding and settling. Pause, speed, cameras, and the settings panel all drive the next run. Replay and Apply rebuild from the current values without changing the selected speed or camera.
