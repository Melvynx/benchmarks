What the app does:

- **Layout:** top bar with the "Claude Opus 5.5" title, search and account menu. A sidebar with a compose button, unread counts, labels, and drag-and-drop targets. On mobile it switches to a drawer.
- **Mailbox:** about 45 seeded conversations with varied senders, labels, read states, attachments, multi-message threads and a calendar invite. It includes 3 drafts, 1 scheduled send and 2 snoozed conversations.
- **Folders:** Inbox with Primary / Promotions / Social / Updates tabs, plus Starred, Snoozed, Important, Sent, Scheduled, Drafts, All Mail, Spam, Trash and user labels.
- **Actions:** select (including shift-click ranges and bulk actions), read/unread, star, important, archive, delete, spam, snooze, move, label and mute. Every destructive action has Undo.
- **Search:** Gmail-style operators such as `from:`, `in:`, `is:` and `has:attachment`, live suggestions, an advanced search panel, filter chips and highlighted matches.
- **Reading:** older messages collapse, and you can view recipient details and quoted text. There's an attachment previewer, RSVP buttons, quick-reply suggestions, newer/older navigation and print.
- **Compose:** floating windows you can stack, minimize or make full screen, plus inline replies and forward. Recipients show as chips with autocomplete and address checks. It also has rich text, attachments, a signature, autosaved drafts, undo send and schedule send.
- **Things happen while it's open:** new mail arrives, contacts reply to what you send them, snoozed mail comes back, and scheduled messages go out on time.
- **Settings:** theme, row density, category tabs, a reading pane beside the inbox, undo-send length, signature and page size. There are also about 30 keyboard shortcuts (press `?`).
- **Storage and mobile:** everything is saved in the browser. On phones, reading and compose are full screen.
