One honest caveat: at the extreme end (24×16, 22 m/s) the rubble field is still slowly slumping at 40 s — ~260 bricks above 0.2 m/s, decaying steadily, holding 110 J out of ~36 kJ of stored potential energy. I checked whether this was solver starvation by doubling velocity and position iterations; it changed nothing, and an undisturbed wall of the same size is perfectly rigid. It's a genuinely tall metastable pile, not numerical creep, so I left the iteration counts alone rather than pay the frame cost for no gain.
