The living-garden simulator is finished. `pnpm build` passes, and a real Chrome run on port 9357 showed no console errors.

**What the browser check covered**
- The browser title and the only `<h1>` are both exactly `Claude Sonnet 5.5`.
- Dragging the dish rotated it, and clicking a blob opened the inspector.
- The charts painted from real history, and the summary reacted to it.

**Simulation settings**
- I kept the energy rules as they were. Eating, contact, copying, mutation, lifespans and the eating and pause locks all run in the model.
- The only tuning change is red movement cost, lowered from 0.04 to 0.02 in `src/sim/engine.ts`.
- With that cost, the balanced garden kept both groups alive in 20 of 24 seeds after 3000 s, and 15 of 16 in an earlier run. The old value gave 13 of 16.
- The balanced garden is not guaranteed to survive: about 1 in 6 seeds still ends with one group, or both, dying out. The run summary says so when it happens.
- I also added a blue crowding-stress knob, but it made no clear difference, so it stays at 0.

**Presets**
I ran each preset for 1800 s over 4 seeds:

| Preset | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Balanced | Both groups alive in all 4 seeds, with repeated waves. |
| Fruit-rich | Both groups alive in all 4 seeds, with large waves. |
| Red-heavy | Both groups alive in 1 of 4 seeds. |
| Fragile | Collapsed in 3 of 4 seeds, and 1 seed lost all reds. |
| Low-fruit | Blue stays around 20 and red fades. |

**Finishing edits**
- I removed the `window.__garden` debug hook.
- I tightened the run-summary verdicts. "Low fruit kept the garden small" and "Stayed balanced" only appear after enough garden time. Before that, the summary says "Still finding its rhythm".

**Cleanup**
- My Portly tuning jobs had all exited. I stopped my dev-server job, so nothing of mine is running.
- Nothing was deleted, so `.tmp/` and the experiment scripts are still there.
- `playwright-core` is a dev dependency I added only for these checks. `dist/` is the output of `pnpm build`.

**One thing to know**
Chrome and Node do not produce identical runs for the same seed, probably because of small float differences in their math functions. Seed 7 lost its reds in Node but kept them in Chrome.

**Run it**
`pnpm dev` serves on `http://127.0.0.1:9357`.
