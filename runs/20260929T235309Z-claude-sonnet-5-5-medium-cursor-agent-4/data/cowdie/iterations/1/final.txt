**The app.** It's a 3D cow pet you feed and care for, with the browser title and main heading both set to "Claude Sonnet 5.5":
- **Feeding:** Apple, hay, carrot and cake each drop into the bowl. Cowdie walks over, lowers her head and takes three bites with crumbs and munch sounds. The food shrinks, the belly bar rises, and she hops with hearts. When she's stuffed she shakes her head and refuses. Too much cake gives her a tummy ache.
- **Care:** Drag over her to pet her, or tap to poke. She chases a ball you can throw, takes a bubble bath, sleeps, and wears unlockable outfits.
- **Progress:** Her four stats decay over time, she levels up with XP, and everything is saved in the browser.
- **Her behaviour:** Her eyes follow your pointer, she wanders, grazes and moos, and she has sound effects and moods.
