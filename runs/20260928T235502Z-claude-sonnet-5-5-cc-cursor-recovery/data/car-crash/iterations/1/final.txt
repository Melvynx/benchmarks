What I actually ran:

- **Stability across the range:** 18 crash runs covered min and max speed, brick mass and wall size, plus extremes in car mass, gravity, restitution and friction. None produced NaN, tunneling through the wall or a runaway blow-up.

- **Defaults and settings:** the default values load exactly as specified, and editing one setting changed only that setting. Out-of-range values are clamped, and Restore defaults works.
