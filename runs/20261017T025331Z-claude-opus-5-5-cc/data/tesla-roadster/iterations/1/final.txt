I rebuilt the ramp to match the frames and retuned the stunt around it. **The new ramp**
- **Size:** 40 m tall (about ten car lengths), 8 m wide, with a 0.6 m thick slab.
- **Shape:** one continuous curve, with the curvature changing smoothly the whole way. It starts flat on the ground and bends up through vertical, reaching it about 24 m up. It then bends over the top into a nearly flat ceiling that ends at 165°. The ceiling past 150° is 11 m long, plenty for the 4.5 m car. The tightest bend has a 9.9 m radius, so the bumpers never scrape.
- **Overhang:** the lip ends almost directly above where the curve starts, about 17 m back from the wall, which gives the hook shape you see in frames 02, 22 and 23.
- **Truss:** two 45 m lattice towers behind the wall, a 3 m deep top truss reaching out past the lip with hangers down to the overhanging top, struts tying the wall to the towers, and posts under the lower bend. Ground light strips surround the base, as in frame 23.

**Changes to the physics, controller and defaults**
- **Default thrust is now 90 kN** (was 64). The car falls 38 m before landing, and 64 kN can't brake that safely.
- **Timing:** the climb takes 6.1 s and the car hangs at 163° for exactly 2.0 s. From release to touchdown takes 5.6 s, including about 1.3 s of hover just above the ground. That's longer than the roughly 2 s in the clip, but a 38 m drop takes 2.8 s even in free fall.
- **"Too slow" works differently now.** On a 40 m wall a stalled car can only stall below vertical, and with its fans cut it just slid back down the curve undamaged, so cutting power no longer produced a crash. Instead the controller now fires its normal release from wherever the car stalled. At the preset 78 kW the car is still against the wall when it rolls, hits the concrete and tumbles down. At some other power values (around 60 and 85–95 kW) it drops clear and lands on its jets, and the banner then says "Aborted below the lip".
- **Cameras and Director:** every camera preset is repositioned for the bigger structure, and the Director stays on the drone shot longer during the fall. I also reduced the fog, enlarged the concrete texture for the longer ramp, moved the floodlights, fixed the flat start of the ramp flickering against the ground in distant shots, and moved a light cart that was blocking the close-on-lip camera.
