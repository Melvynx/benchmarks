I've written `index.html` in the working directory. It is the only file there, about 900 lines and self-contained. It passes a syntax check, but I haven't run it in a browser, so none of the physics, rendering or controls has been seen working. Expect the crash to need tuning once you open it.

**Checks I ran:**
- The module script passes `node --check`.
- The import map is valid JSON.
- The file starts with `<!DOCTYPE html>` and ends with `</html>`.
- The title is exactly "Claude Opus 5.5", and that is the only brand on the page.
- There are no `Math.random` calls.

**What's in it:**
- **Physics:** Three.js 0.160 and Rapier 0.14, both pinned on jsDelivr. The sim runs at a fixed 1/120 s step, at most 6 steps per frame, with 8 solver iterations. CCD (anti-tunneling) is on for every moving body.
- **Crash:** the car speeds up by itself on load, down a 40 m straight, reaching 22 m/s just before a running-bond wall of 12×8×2 real brick bodies. The bricks have visible mortar gaps and half bricks at the ends of alternate rows.
- **Car:** 23 separate bodies linked by joints that break when the contact and inertial load on them passes a force limit. Breaking is only possible after 0.25 s, and ground contact never counts toward it.
  - The front clip crumples in two stages and can detach once it's fully crushed.
  - The hood is two hinged panels that buckle up or fly off when their latch breaks.
  - The trunk, the 4 doors (each with a hinge and a latch) and both bumpers are separate parts.
  - The windshield, rear window and 4 side windows shatter into small glass pieces that are themselves physics bodies.
  - The 4 wheels have rims and tires and can come off and roll.
- **Playback:** Pause/Play (Space also toggles, unless a number field is focused) and speeds of 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2×. Speed only changes the clock, and the chosen speed and camera stay the same across Replay and Apply.
- **Cameras:** Impact (the default), Follow, Top and Orbit. Orbit rotates on its own until you drag.
- **Settings:** number inputs for every required default, plus Apply and Defaults buttons. Replay also reads the inputs and rebuilds the run.
- **Rendering:** everything the brief asked for — PBR materials, 2048 PCFSoft sun shadows, contact blob shadows, RoomEnvironment reflections, ACES, and the full track and scenery.
- **Effects:** dust, sparks, camera shake and skid marks run on their own seeded random generator, so they can't change the physics.

The break limits, crumple stiffness and camera framing are my estimates, not tested values. Two library assumptions are also unconfirmed:
- that Rapier's `RigidBodyDesc.setSleeping` exists, which the bricks use so they start asleep;
- that three.js provides `RoundedBoxGeometry` at the path I import.

If either is wrong, the page will fail on load, so check those first if it doesn't start.

Separately, the `chatcut` and `posthog` tool connections need you to sign in (in the claude.ai connector settings, or `/mcp` in an interactive session) before they can be used. They weren't needed for this task.
