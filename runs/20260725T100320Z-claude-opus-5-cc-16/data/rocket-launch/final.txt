**On the 60fps requirement — one caveat you should know.** This environment's Chromium reports `no-webgl` even headed, so canvas is software-rasterized and I cannot measure the GPU path at all. Isolated frame times here are 26 ms at 1100×720 and 52–63 ms at 1440×900. Ablation shows smoke is ~93% of that cost, which is pure fill rate — the thing a real GPU makes cheap. So I expect 60fps on actual hardware, but I could not prove it here. What I did do is make the scene shed load on its own: a 4.2M-pixel backing-store budget, emission rates scaled by a frame-time-driven quality knob down to 0.30, the alpha cull above, and the heat haze gated off below 0.45 quality.
