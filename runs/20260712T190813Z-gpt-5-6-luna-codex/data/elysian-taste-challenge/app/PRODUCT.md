# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who collect beautiful objects, visit cultural spaces, and look for sensory brands with a strong point of view. They are encountering Elysian in a curious, unhurried moment and want to understand the world before they decide whether to step further into it.

## Product Purpose

Elysian is a fictional premium house for botanical rituals and luminous objects. The landing page should create immediate desire, establish a memorable visual world, and invite a visitor to explore the house at their own pace.

## Brand Personality

Imaginative, cultivated, slightly unruly. The voice is observant and sensorial, with the confidence to leave some things unresolved. The experience should feel like walking into a greenhouse after dark and finding something that has just bloomed.

## Anti-references

Avoid startup landing-page scaffolding, feature grids, generic luxury beige, interchangeable editorial magazine layouts, obvious wellness language, and polished-but-empty component-library surfaces. Elysian should not feel like a SaaS template, a perfume storefront, or a monochrome fashion lookbook.

## Design Principles

- Let the botanical image behave like an environment, not a framed asset.
- Give visitors one strong idea per viewport and let the pacing do some of the selling.
- Pair cultivated typography with a flash of the unexpected.
- Make interaction reveal personality, not just confirm affordances.
- Keep the writing specific, tactile, and a little strange.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for essential text and controls. Keep navigation and content understandable without animation, provide keyboard-visible focus states, respect reduced-motion preferences, and ensure the experience remains legible and usable on small screens and touch devices.
