`index.html` is written in the app directory and passes a JavaScript syntax check. I did not run or view the page, since the task forbids opening a browser or running the simulation.

The previous session left no files, so I wrote the whole page from scratch. The file starts with `<!DOCTYPE html>` and ends with `</html>`. The title and the only visible brand are "Claude Sonnet 5.5". It is a single static file, so there was no server, port, `pnpm` or Portly step. The original prompt names no port, so there was none to keep.

**What's in it**
- **Libraries:** Three.js 0.160 and cannon-es 0.20 from a pinned CDN import map.
- **Scene:** a 40 m track with asphalt, lane markings, a start area, curbs, gravel shoulders, barriers, lights and a sky.
- **Wall:** 12 columns × 8 rows × 2 deep of individual rigid bricks in a staggered bond, with visible mortar gaps. It starts asleep so it stands still until hit.
- **Car:** the car breaks apart by:
  - Parts: tub with roof, front clip in two segments plus bumper, rear clip and bumper, hood, trunk, four doors, four glass panes, and four wheels on hinges.
  - Constraints: parts are held by breakable constraints that only start breaking after the first car–brick contact. A break needs the constraint force to stay above its threshold for two steps in a row.
  - Glass: a broken pane becomes 8–15 shard bodies each.
- **Driving:** a controller applies force to the car so it reaches the impact speed at the wall, and stops once contact starts. If the run is too short for 3 g of acceleration, the car starts with an initial velocity instead.
- **Physics:** fixed timestep (1/240 s, or 1/480 s above 30 m/s) with a cap on steps per frame, so playback speed only changes the clock. Randomness is seeded and used only for visuals and shard spawn.
- **Rendering:** ACES tone mapping, a 4096 soft PCF sun shadow with texel snapping, a sky environment map generated in code, clearcoat paint, glass, rough bricks, and contact blobs under every body. Impact effects are dust, sparks, skid marks and camera shake.
- **Controls:** Pause/Play (also Space), Replay, speeds 0.25×/0.5×/1×/2×, and the Impact/Follow/Top/Orbit views. A settings panel has 17 numeric inputs for speed, mass, car size, wall counts and brick size, gravity, friction, restitution and track length. Apply and Replay both rebuild from the panel values. Speed and view survive a rebuild.

**Risks I could not check**
- **Break thresholds:** they are set from reasoning about impact forces, not tuned. They may break too much or too little at the defaults.
- **Wall and car stability:** brick-wall stability in cannon-es is untested.
- **Camera framing:** it is untested.
