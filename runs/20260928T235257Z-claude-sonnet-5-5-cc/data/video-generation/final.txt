The 45-second Lumail launch video is done, saved as `video.mp4` with a minimal `index.html` player next to it. I opened the player in headless Chrome at three viewport sizes and the video played at each.

**Checks**
- **MP4:** 1920×1080 H.264, 30 fps, exactly 45.000 s, about 4.8 MB.
- **Player:** In headless Chrome at 1920×1080, 1000×900 and 1400×500, the video played with no errors and its box stayed at exactly 16:9. It fills the full width or height, letterboxed with black, with no cropping or distortion.
- **Encoded frames:** I pulled frames from the MP4 itself at 4.2 s (a transition), 29 s and 44.9 s. The 29 s frame is the migration scene mid-animation, so the stat counters still read 0 there.
- **Player page:** The browser title is "Claude Sonnet 5.5" and there is no visible heading. Native playback controls are on.

**Video**
- **Research:** I used public sources only: lumail.io, its stylesheet, and web search. The design matches the site: warm off-white background, black Geist type, an orange-red `#ff3300` accent with a 3D button, white pill highlights, and the red "L" logo.
- **Story:** The video runs through these scenes:
  1. Hook: "Email marketing shouldn't feel like a second job."
  2. Brand reveal: "The AI Tool that will make you love e-mail marketing."
  3. An AI agent tagging 312 subscribers.
  4. The email editor.
  5. A workflow sequence.
  6. Migrating from Mailchimp and others.
  7. Transactional email with a priority lane.
  8. Pricing: "You pay for emails sent. Never for subscribers."
  9. "3M+ emails sent" with testimonials.
  10. An end card with "Start free · 3,000 emails/month · no card · lumail.io."
- **Transitions:** Orange and black wipes join the scenes.

The video has no audio track. The frames were rendered from HTML with Chrome and encoded with ffmpeg. The build files are in `work/`, but `video.mp4` and `index.html` don't need them.
