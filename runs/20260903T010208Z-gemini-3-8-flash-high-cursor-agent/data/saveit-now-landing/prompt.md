# Redesign the SaveIt.now landing page

You are working in a self-contained Vite, React, and TypeScript application. It contains the former SaveIt.now landing page, whose hero uses a dark orange gradient, plus six original campaign images in `public/images/landing-v2/`.

Redesign only this landing page into a complete, premium, modern, new-generation product site. Audit every supplied image before deciding how to use it, then use the set purposefully throughout the experience rather than treating the images as a generic gallery. The visual language should feel cinematic, energetic, editorial, and highly crafted, with the kind of confident art direction associated with a major contemporary consumer brand campaign. Keep SaveIt.now recognizable and make the page feel cohesive from hero through footer.

SaveIt.now is "your home for bookmarks." Its differentiated promise has two connected sides:

1. Agentic bookmarking: a user only saves something. The product handles capture, understanding, categorization, organization, and retrieval without manual filing.
2. A polished iOS experience: saving and rediscovering should feel immediate and native wherever the user is.

The central idea is simple: no manual work, just "Save it." The page should make visitors believe they can retrieve what they saved instead of losing it in folders, tabs, or forgotten bookmark lists. Express the strong "100% promise" as an aspirational product promise about rediscovery and confidence, without inventing unsupported statistics, testimonials, guarantees, or urgency.

Use conversion-focused copywriting. Lead with one clear promise, sell the lived transformation before listing features, and keep the reading rhythm concise. Do not use em dashes anywhere in the copy. Avoid vague phrases that do not explain the product. All visible product copy must be in English.

The finished page must include:

- a distinctive hero with a strong headline, concise support copy, primary CTA, secondary CTA, and a premium campaign-style use of the supplied imagery
- a clear explanation of the agentic workflow and the "just save it" mechanism
- a visually memorable section for the 100% rediscovery promise
- an iOS section that presents the mobile side credibly without fabricating a fake screenshot of the actual app
- pricing with a free tier and a $5 per month Pro tier billed annually
- an elegant, interactive FAQ treatment
- a polished closing CTA and footer

Preserve or improve useful interactions. Add purposeful motion and responsive behavior, with reduced-motion support. Make navigation and calls to action functional. Ensure the design works at desktop and mobile widths, remains accessible, and has no obvious overflow or unreadable contrast. Keep the implementation maintainable and use the existing stack.
