I built the Gmail-style mail app (React, TypeScript and Vite). It runs on port 9227 under Portly, and the page title and the visible logo both read "Claude Sonnet 5.5". `tsc` and `pnpm build` pass, and I saw no console errors.

In dev-browser on desktop I checked:
- the inbox and category tabs
- opening a thread and sending an inline reply
- sending a new message from the compose window
- `has:attachment from:priya` search
- keyboard shortcuts (j, x, e, z)
- snoozing a message
- dark theme

On a 390px mobile viewport I checked the inbox list, opening a thread, and the compose screen. I did not test bulk labeling, scheduled send, settings, or attachment previews.

- **Mailbox:** about 33 seeded conversations with attachments, labels, threads and drafts. Views cover Inbox (with category tabs), Starred, Snoozed, Sent, Drafts, Scheduled, All Mail, Spam, Trash and labels. Star, read/unread, archive, delete, spam, snooze, labeling and bulk selection all work, with Undo toasts.
- **Search:** live suggestions as you type, an advanced-filter panel, and operators (`from:`, `to:`, `subject:`, `has:attachment`, `is:unread`, `label:`, `in:`, `newer_than:`).
- **Reading:** collapsed and expanded messages, reply / reply all / forward inline, attachment preview and download.
- **Compose:** recipient chips with contact suggestions, Cc/Bcc, a rich-text editor, attachments including drag-and-drop, and draft autosave. Send has Undo and a schedule option. Contacts sometimes send a simulated reply after a delay, which you can turn off in settings.
- **Other:** light, dark and system themes, density, a mobile drawer with a compose button, and state saved in localStorage. Settings also cover labels, signature and undo delay, and a "Reset demo mailbox" button restores the seed data.

One rough edge: on a fresh compose, the caret can land after the signature rather than above it. The search box also keeps its text after you open a thread from the suggestions dropdown.
