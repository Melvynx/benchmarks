**Settings I kept.** From the completed sweeps, the most robust combination is:
- **Red territory:** a red only buds if at most one other red is within 5 units.
- **Satiation:** a full red stops hunting.
- **Hungry lurking:** hungry reds sit and wait more instead of roaming.
- **Shade:** blues eating or waiting under a canopy are only visible at 55% of a red's vision.

This kept reds alive in 7 of 8 seeds over 2 simulated hours, with about 8 waves per seed. I left "search image" (reds' vision shrinking between catches) switched off, and its code is inert. It stopped the fruit-rich crash but made balanced reds die out, 1 of 8 surviving at full strength.

**Presets, checked on real runs (seeds 7 and 8, 30 sim-minutes each):**

| Preset | What happened |
|---|---|
| Balanced | 2–3 waves, reds peak about 3.5–4.5 min after blues, both groups alive |
| Fruit-rich | Blues go from 30 to 200+ in about 2 min. Seed 7 kept cycling; seed 8 went fully extinct |
| Red-heavy | Blues dip first, then reds fall to about half; both groups persist |
| Fragile | Harder hugs, hungrier reds; bigger swings, both alive at 30 min |
| Low-fruit | About 20 blues; reds die out within 3 minutes |

- **Fruit-rich crash:** when blues boom, reds boom behind them and can wipe them out. The preset's description says so; I didn't hide it.
- **Fragile:** these settings weren't in any sweep. Only the two 30-minute runs above back them, so they may be less robust over longer runs than the other presets.
