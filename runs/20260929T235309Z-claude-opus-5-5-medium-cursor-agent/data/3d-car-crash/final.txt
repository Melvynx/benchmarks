**Controls:** Replay, Pause/Play, speed 0.25× / 0.5× / 1× / 2×, and views Impact / Follow / Top / Orbit (Orbit auto-rotates until you drag). The settings panel has all 17 required fields. Apply, Replay or Enter in a field rebuilds the run from the current values, and speed and view carry over across rebuilds. Keys: Space pauses unless a number field is focused, R replays, 1–4 switch views.
