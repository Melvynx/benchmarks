I built the experience as one file, `index.html`, but I could not see the 3D scene running, so it is untested in a real WebGL browser.

The only browser available here has no WebGL. In it the page loads with the title "Claude Sonnet 5.5" and shows the WebGL-unavailable fallback message. The script also passes a Node syntax check. I did not run the controls, the guide, the labels or the visuals.

- **Scene:** a cutaway of a room and the outdoors separated by one wall, with a split system and a monoblock, each in Cooling and Heating.
- **Controls:** Split System / Monoblock, Cooling / Heating, Play/Pause, Reset View, Replay Guide, Labels and Key.
- **Guide:** 8 timed steps with fly-to camera moves, a highlight on the component being explained, and previous/next buttons.
- **Panels:** the live state panel and the legend.
- **Fallbacks and layout:** it uses Three.js from a CDN, loads no images or other external files, and has a mobile layout and reduced-motion handling.

Open it in Chrome or Safari with hardware acceleration on to check it.
