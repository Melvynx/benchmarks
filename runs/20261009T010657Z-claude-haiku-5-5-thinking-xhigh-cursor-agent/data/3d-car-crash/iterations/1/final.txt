What's in it:
- **Scene:** 40 m track with lane paint, curbs, gravel, barriers, light poles, sky dome, ACES tone mapping, a 2048 PCF soft sun shadow, RoomEnvironment reflections, and contact blobs under bodies.
- **Wall:** 192 individual cannon-es bricks in a running bond with visible mortar gaps.
- **Car:** 22 rigid parts: split front clip, hood, trunk, four doors, two bumpers, windshield, four side windows, four wheels with rims and tires, and headlights and tail lights.
- **Breakage:** 22 bonds, each a point-to-point constraint with a force limit in newtons (`BOND_SPEC`). Glass shatters into a grid of shard bodies. Parts that overlap at spawn are excluded from collision so the car does not explode on frame 1.
- **Playback:** fixed 240 Hz step, at most 8 steps per frame, Pause/Play, and 0.25×/0.5×/1×/2×. Space toggles pause unless a number input has focus.
- **Cameras:** Impact, Follow, Top, and Orbit (OrbitControls). Switching is an immediate cut.
- **Settings:** every field writes to `settings` on Apply or Replay, clamped to limits. Defaults are the mandatory values.
- **FX:** dust, sparks, skid marks, and camera shake. FX use a seeded RNG and sim time, so they stay in step with pause and slow motion.

Assumptions and gaps:
- Bond force reads the cannon-es solver's `multiplier`. I believe that's the per-step impulse, but I couldn't verify it. If it's wrong, the fallback uses constraint separation times stiffness, and the thresholds will be off.
- Static environment meshes are not merged yet.
